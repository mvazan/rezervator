# Služby na kantýně na kiosku

Requested by the user on 2026-10-10/11. The app speaks Czech; the user writes Slovak. Branch `kiosk-duty` from `main` (3bd1d13, migrations up to 0065 there); migration `0073_kiosk_duty.sql` (0066–0072 are taken by the parallel kiosk branches — the second branch to merge renumbers, never `db push --include-all`).

## Why

The app shows the canteen duty (0050, `duty_periods` + `duty_assignments`) as a line under the week range: „Služba: Jan Novák a Petr Svoboda“, or per part of the week „Služba: po–st Jan Novák · čt–ne Petr Svoboda“. The kiosk has no week: its board starts today (minus the admin's past days) and runs on, and duty periods have arbitrary bounds. A visitor at the kiosk wants two answers: **who serves now** (whom to ask in the canteen — the booking note even sends people there) and **when do I serve** (the plan on the wall).

## Decided with the user

Three places, each switched on separately by the admin (Správa → Kiosk → new section „Služby na kantýně“), all off by default:

1. **V záhlaví (header)** — under the clock and the date in the status bar: a compact tinted line „☕ Služba: Jan Novák a Petr Svoboda“ (today's duty). A tap opens the dialog „Služby na kantýně“: the current duty highlighted, then the next ones in date order.
2. **V kalendáři (board)** — a band across the day columns' headers on the kiosk board, like an all-day event: one tinted segment per day of a period, rounded only where the period starts/ends. The names are **one sticky label per period**, like a multi-day event's title in Google Calendar (asked for on 2026-10-11 — the first version put them on the period's first day and on the column where the scroll last settled, so while scrolling they showed twice or vanished for a moment): the label rides the period's start while that is in view, sticks to the left edge of the header row while the period runs off to the left (a period that began before the board's first visible day still says who), and where the visible part of the period (from its start or the row's left edge, whichever is further right, to its end) is narrower than the label, the label still starts there and is **cut with an ellipsis** at the period's end („Jana Nováková a…“, „Jana…“; asked for on 2026-10-11 — pushing it left by the end let the row's edge cut it in the middle of a name, even at rest, e.g. a weekly duty on Sunday with Sunday as the first column: „ovák a Petr Svoboda“; a period narrower than the label kept it at its start, so once its first day scrolled off only the middle showed). Only when the room left falls below about two glyphs and the ellipsis (~24 px, measured in the label's style and text size) does the period's end push the label out to the left (never past its rounded corner), so it still leaves continuously as the period scrolls away. Nothing appears or disappears during a scroll — the label moves continuously, frame by frame, also in the middle of a drag. A tap on the band opens the same dialog. Where in the headers it runs is the admin's choice (asked for on 2026-10-11): above the date, between the date and the day's matches (the default, the first version's place) or at the bottom under the matches.
3. **V panelu (drawer)** — a card „SLUŽBA NA KANTÝNĚ“ in the side drawer with the current duty large and the next ones listed, taking the admin's share of the drawer's height (a % like the notices' share).

The modes combine freely (independent switches — the admin may pick exactly one or several).

## Domain (pure, unit-tested — `lib/domain/duties.dart`)

- `KioskDutyEntry` — a period and its assignees' display names, Czech-sorted (`compareCzech`). A period with no known assignee is not an entry (the app's header skips unassigned periods too).
- `kioskDutyPlan(periods, assignments, names, today, {ahead = 5})` → `({KioskDutyEntry? now, List<KioskDutyEntry> next})`: `now` = the period covering today (a period ending today still runs — `splitDuties`' rule), `next` = the ones starting after today, date order, at most `ahead`.
- `kioskDutyOn(periods, assignments, names, day)` → `({KioskDutyEntry entry, bool starts, bool ends})?` for the board's band: the period covering `day`, whether `day` is its first/last day.

Labels reuse `dutyRangeLabel`, `dutyDayLabel`, `joinNames`.

## The kiosk

- **Provider:** `kioskDutyPlanProvider` (kiosk feature) from `dutyPeriodsProvider`, `dutyAssignmentsProvider`, `playersProvider` (id → display name) and `nowProvider`.
- **Header (mode 1):** in `KioskShell`'s `_StatusBar`, under the clock column's date: a line on `secondaryContainer` (`onSecondaryContainer` text), `Icons.local_cafe_outlined`, „Služba: <names>“, one line, ellipsis, at most ~420 px wide. Only when mode 1 is on and someone serves today; otherwise nothing. A tap → `showKioskDutyDialog` (through the shell, the touch counts for the idle timer like the notice dialog; the idle reset's `popUntil` closes it).
- **Dialog:** title „Služby na kantýně“; the current duty as a tinted block „Teď slouží: <names>“ + its range; „Další“ and up to 5 rows „<range> — <names>“ (range left, names right, wrapping); none ahead → „Další služby zatím nejsou naplánované.“; „Zavřít“. No current duty → the block is left out.
- **Board (mode 2):** `BoardColumnHeader` (shared with the app's week board — the app passes nothing and stays unchanged) gains an optional band segment (`band`) and where the band runs (`bandPosition`, null = no band, no room for one); `KioskBoardView` passes each day's piece when mode 2 is on — a `BoardBandSegment` from `kioskDutyOn(...)`: the day's index in the period (`day`, negative columns allowed — a period begun before the board) and the period's length (`days`; `starts`/`ends` follow from them), who serves (`label`, the same on every piece), the header list's `ScrollController` (`_hHeader`, which mirrors the board's scroll) and the piece's column — and the admin's `kioskDutyBandPosition`. The header grows by the strip's height (`boardBandHeight`) while mode 2 is on, the same for every position. Where the strip runs: `top` — on the header's top edge, the day label under it (a day without a duty keeps the room, so the labels stay on one line); `middle` — between the day label and the match lines (the default, the first version's place); `bottom` — on the header's bottom edge under the match lines, there however many lines the day has (the header's height is fixed, the room the lines leave stays above the band). In each position the pieces meet through the gaps between the headers. Segment: `secondaryContainer`, radius only on the start/end sides. Names (`labelSmall`, w600, one line, ellipsis): `stickyBandLabel(viewportLeft, bandStart, bandEnd, labelWidth, padding = 6, minWidth = 24)` → `({double left, double width})` in `calendar_board.dart` (pure, unit-tested, continuous in `viewportLeft`) places the one label in the header list's scroll space and gives the width it is drawn at (the label's own, or the room before the period's end — the `Text` laid out that wide shows the ellipsis) (`bandStart`/`bandEnd` = the period's first/last column × column width ± the piece's 6 px inset, `viewportLeft` = the header scroll offset); every piece draws the whole label at its local offset (label left − its strip's left), clipped to its strip including the bleed through the gap, and only where the label crosses it — so one text reads across the day boundaries. The label is laid over the strip (not inside its `Material`) with a hard-edge clip, which it may use because it always keeps 6 px clear of the rounded ends; the strip below takes the tap. On scroll only the label rebuilds (a `ListenableBuilder` on `_hHeader` inside the piece); the strip, the header and the board do not — the old `_firstVisible`/`_settledOn` rebuild on scroll end is gone (`onVisibleDays` keeps its own computation). The label's width and the minimum (its first two glyphs and „…“) come from a `TextPainter` in the same style and the reader's `MediaQuery` text scale. Hidden in the collapsed header. A tap on the strip opens the dialog (its own gesture — the rest of the header keeps opening the day's matches).
- **Drawer (mode 3):** a card like `_NoticesCard`/`_MatchesCard` (`_Card`, `Icons.local_cafe_outlined`, title „SLUŽBA NA KANTÝNĚ“): the current duty („Teď slouží“, names large, range) or „Dnes nikdo neslouží.“, then „Další“ rows; scrolls if it overflows. It stands at the top of the drawer with `flex: kioskDutyShare`, the existing notices/matches column below with `flex: 100 − share` (their own split unchanged); alone it fills the drawer. `KioskPanelContent` gains the duty so a drawer with only the duty card exists. While a match is being played the live view takes the whole drawer, as today.
- **Admin:** section „Služby na kantýně“ after „Nástěnka“, about „Kdo právě slouží na kantýně a kdo přijde na řadu — podle plánu ve Správě → Služby. Každé místo se zapíná zvlášť.“; switches „V záhlaví“ („Pod hodinami „Služba: …“ s dnešní službou; ťuk ukáže plán.“), „V kalendáři“ („Pás se jmény přes dny služby v hlavičkách dnů tabule.“; while it is on, right under it the list „Kde je pás“ with „Nad datem“ (`top`), „Mezi datem a zápasy“ (`middle`, the default) and „Dole pod zápasy“ (`bottom`), PATCHing `kiosk_duty_band_position` like the notices' list), „V panelu“ („Karta se službou a plánem v postranním panelu.“) and, with the drawer on, the number „Podíl karty v panelu“ (%, 10–90; helper „Kolik výšky panelu karta zabere; zbytek si dělí nástěnka a zápasy.“). Through `_switch` / `_number` / `_panel`.
