# Stream zápasu na kiosku (YouTube)

Requested by the user on 2026-10-10 after the kiosk moved onto an Android display (PR #192). The app speaks Czech; the user writes Slovak. Migration `0066_kiosk_stream.sql` (main is at 0065).

## Decided with the user

- A **floating window** on the kiosk with the match's YouTube stream — no YouTube app, an embedded player. Movable, resizable, maximizable to the whole screen, collapsible; **the idle reset leaves it where the visitor put it** (it resets the name picker, the drawer and the board, never the window).
- **YouTube only, no admin-typed address:** the links already come with the matches — `priority_slots.video_url` from the federation site (`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…`, 15 matches have one in production). The kiosk streams the match that is on now.
- Works on the web kiosk and in the Android app (the APK on the display).

## Why

The alley streams its home matches on YouTube; the display on the wall should show the stream beside the board while the match is played, without anybody at the display having to open or find anything.

## What the kiosk does

1. **Which matches stream** (pure Dart, `kioskStreamMatches` in `lib/domain/kiosk_panel.dart`): the alley's own federation matches (`type.isMatch`, `parentId == null`, `fromFederation`, like the drawer's lists) whose `videoUrl` carries a YouTube video id and that are **on air**: `isLive(slot, result, now)` (the gate `refresh_match` and the kiosk's live view already share: from an hour before the start until the site says finished, at most 12 h), **or** decided (finished/forfeit) and `now` within `[start − 1 h, min(result.decidedAt + 30 min, planned end + 2 h))` — the closing minutes of a stream run on after the site stamps the result. `match_results.decided_at` (0066) is when the server FIRST saw the match decided, stamped once by `apply_federation_result` and never moved (`fetched_at` moves with every re-read — the app's ⟳ on a finished match would reopen the window hours later; rows from before 0066 fall back to `fetched_at`). A match that overruns its planned end keeps its stream, one decided early does not show YouTube's end screen for an hour. The lower edge keeps a forfeit the site stores days ahead from opening the window early; the upper edge keeps a nightly re-read from reopening it at two in the morning. Chronological, like `kioskLiveMatches`. Not gated on the players' data (`withData`): a stream starts before the first throw.
2. **One window, the first match in it.** With two or more streaming at once the title bar shows „1/2“ with ‹ › to turn between them; the window keeps its place and size while turning.
3. **The window appears** when the first streaming match shows up — at the admin's corner and width — and **goes away** when none streams any more (the match is over). A visitor cannot close it; collapsing is the way to get it out of the way. The window is ONE widget that stays mounted for as long as any match streams (never keyed by the set of matches): when a second match joins or the shown one ends, the window keeps its place, size and collapsed/maximized state and only the player's video changes when the shown match changes (the player is keyed by the video id, so turning to another match reloads only the player).
4. **Lives above the board and the drawer** (a `Stack` in `KioskShell`, under the status bar), its own `RepaintBoundary`. The shell's idle `Listener` sees touches on the window's frame (they count as touching the kiosk); touches inside the player go to the player (an iframe, a WebView) and the kiosk never sees them — the idle reset may fire while someone taps the player, which resets the rest of the kiosk and leaves the window alone.
5. **The player is switched off for touches whenever Flutter must win them** (`KioskStreamView.interactive`): while a drag or resize of the window runs, and while any route sits above the shell — the name picker, a notice, a day's matches from the board's header, the booking confirm, the Zápis. On the web the engine keeps every canvas at `pointer-events: none` and only the platform-view slot answers, so a dialog drawn over the iframe would otherwise be see-through to YouTube exactly where they overlap (a name tile over the window would pause the video instead of picking the name) and a mouse drag crossing the player would lose its pointer; the web player therefore sets the iframe's own `pointer-events` to `none` while `interactive` is false. On Android Flutter hit-tests the WebView itself and a modal barrier already covers it: the io player ignores the flag. The shell asks its own route rather than counting dialogs: the stream slot hands the layer `interactive: ModalRoute.isCurrentOf(context) ?? true`. Every dialog and route above the kiosk (`showNamePicker`, `showDialog`, `showDayMatchesDialog`, `showKioskZapis`, the board's booking `confirmDialog`, anything added later) is pushed on the root navigator above the shell's route, which stops being current while one is up; `isCurrentOf` depends on the `isCurrent` aspect of the route's `_ModalScopeStatus` (an `InheritedModel`), and the route's `didChangeNext`/`didPopNext` rebuild it on the push and the pop — the stream slot rebuilds (a `Builder`), not the board. The window adds its own gesture state.
6. **Muted autoplay.** Browsers and WebViews allow autoplay only without sound; the visitor unmutes in the player's own control. The display's speakers are the player's business, not the kiosk's.
7. **No way out of the kiosk through the player.** The embed's title, logo, „Watch on YouTube“ and share controls are links out; on the web the iframe is sandboxed without `allow-popups`/`allow-top-navigation` (`sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-presentation"`), so they die silently; on Android a `NavigationDelegate` lets through only the host page and `youtube-nocookie.com/embed/` and prevents everything else (webview_flutter_android answers a popup by loading its address in the same WebView unless the delegate refuses).

## The window (`KioskStreamWindow`, `lib/features/kiosk/kiosk_stream_window.dart`)

- **Frame:** a title bar on top (the match's „Domácí – Hosté“, the 1/2 turner when needed, two buttons: „Sbalit“ and „Na celou obrazovku“ / „Zmenšit“), the player below, a thin bottom bar with the resize grip in its right end. Nothing of the frame is ever drawn OVER the player: on the web a Flutter widget above the iframe forces the engine to split the canvas into another layer each frame (the slow display pays for it), so the chrome stays beside the player, not on it.
- **Drag** by the title bar, **resize** by the grip (16:9 kept for the player; the title and bottom bars add their height); minimum width 240 px, the window stays inside the board area (clamped on drag, resize and on a change of the area's size — a drawer opening does not move it, the area is the whole board stack). While a drag or resize runs the player is `interactive: false` (§5) — no Flutter shield over the player, which on the web would be see-through anyway.
- **Maximize** = the whole board area (the status bar stays); **restore** returns to the rect before. **Collapse** = a pill with ▶ and the title at the window's top-left corner; a tap on the pill restores the window where it was. Collapsed or maximized, the player keeps playing (the content widget stays mounted — the same element, moved and resized, never rebuilt from scratch; collapsed, it sits in a 1 × 1 px box behind the pill rather than being removed).
- **Initial place:** the admin's corner (`kiosk_stream_corner`: tl/tr/bl/br, default `bl` — the drawer is on the right) with an 8 px margin, width = `kiosk_stream_width` % of the board area's width (default 35, 20–60), height from 16:9 plus the bars.
- **State** (rect, maximized, collapsed, which match is shown) lives in the window's own `State`: the shell's rebuilds and idle resets keep it, and so does a change of the streaming matches (§3). The shown match is remembered by id; when it leaves the list the window shows the first remaining one.

## The player (`lib/features/kiosk/stream/`)

- `kiosk_stream_view.dart` with a conditional import — `kiosk_stream_view_web.dart` (`dart.library.js_interop`): `HtmlElementView` whose factory (`ui_web.platformViewRegistry.registerViewFactory`, registered once; the view id carries the video id) creates an `<iframe>` with the embed address, `allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture"`, `sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-presentation"`, no `allowfullscreen`, no border, 100 % × 100 %; the element is kept so `interactive` toggles its `style.pointerEvents` (`none`/`auto`). `kiosk_stream_view_io.dart`: `webview_flutter` 4.14 — `WebViewController` with JavaScript on, on Android `AndroidWebViewController.setMediaPlaybackRequiresUserGesture(false)`, a `NavigationDelegate` that prevents every navigation except the host page and `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/`, and **`loadHtmlString` of a one-line host page** (`<iframe src="<embed>" allow="autoplay; encrypted-media" style="border:0;width:100vw;height:100vh">` on a black body) **with `baseUrl: 'https://rezervator.online'`** — a bare `loadRequest` of the embed address would be a top-level load without a Referer, which YouTube refuses since 2025 („Video unavailable“, player error 153); as an iframe of a page with an https origin it plays like on the web. `WebViewWidget`. A different video id = a new element/controller (`ValueKey(videoId)`).
- **Embed address** (`youtubeEmbedUrl` in `lib/domain/youtube.dart`): `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/<id>?autoplay=1&mute=1&playsinline=1&controls=1&fs=0&rel=0&modestbranding=1`. `fs=0`: YouTube's own fullscreen is off — a WebView cannot show it and the window has its own maximize; `rel=0`: at the end no strangers' videos.
- **Video id** (`youtubeVideoId(String url) → String?`): `youtube.com/watch?v=ID` (any other query parameters, `www.`/`m.`), `youtu.be/ID`, `youtube.com/live/ID`, `youtube.com/embed/ID`, `youtube.com/shorts/ID`; an id is 11 characters of `[A-Za-z0-9_-]`; anything else → null (no window for that match). Pure, unit-tested.
- **Injectable for tests:** `kioskStreamContentProvider = Provider<Widget Function(String videoId)>` (default: the platform view); widget tests override it with a `Text(videoId)` — platform views do not run under flutter_test.

## Revision (0072, same day): anyone opens it, recordings, the ×, more windows

The user tried the first version and asked for more: „anyone can switch it on; it closes by itself when the video ends; I want to try several iframes in parallel — a setting for the maximum (at 1 keep the paging); a × to close a stream (with paging it closes only that one); I want to play a recording too.“

- **Anyone opens a stream** with ▶ at a match that has a YouTube video: at each row of the drawer's match list (tooltip „Sledovat živě“ while it is played or an hour before, „Přehrát záznam“ once decided, „Pustit video“ otherwise — `kioskStreamLabel`), beside the live view's freshness line (since: the app's pulsing live badge, tooltip „Živý přenos“ — see below), and in the kiosk's Zápis (a button in its top right corner; it closes the Zápis and opens the stream). A tap on a match on the board or in the header opens its Zápis once it has a score — and, before that, its stream when it has a video. Recordings are simply finished matches with a video.
- **The admin's switch (`kiosk_stream`) only decides whether the match on air opens by itself** („Živý přenos se otevře sám“); the corner, the width and the new count are always shown.
- **The × closes the stream shown** (in the title bar and on the collapsed pill); with several in one window the window turns on to the next. A stream closed by the × — or by its video's end — never opens by itself again; its ▶ opens it (`KioskStreams.closed` in `kioskStreamsProvider`, in memory: a restart of the kiosk forgets it).
- **The end of the video closes it:** the embed loads with `enablejsapi=1&origin=<page origin>`; the page says „listening“ and subscribes to `onStateChange` (the iframe protocol of YouTube's IFrame API, repeated every second until the player answers, ten times at most); state 0 closes the window. Web: one `message` listener for every iframe, messages from `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com` matched by the id in the hello (the platform view id). App: the host page's script does the same and passes state changes to the JavaScript channel `KioskStream`. The parsing (`youtubeHello`, `youtubePlayerId`, `youtubeEndedId`) is pure Dart, unit-tested.
- **More windows** (`kiosk_stream_max` 1–4, default 1, migration 0072): at 1 one window turns through every open stream (‹ n/m ›); with more, each stream has its own window, the first in the admin's corner, then the one above or below it, the one beside it, the one across — the same side first, the drawer's side last (`KioskCorner.fromHere`; a window takes the lowest corner free when it opens). What visitors opened comes first: when their streams fill the windows the live one gives way (it comes back once one is closed); a ▶ past the count closes the oldest opened (`kioskVisibleStreams`, `KioskStreamsNotifier.open`). Windows keep their order in the stack for as long as their stream is open — an iframe moved in the page would reload. While one window is maximized the others hide (their players run on in a 1 × 1 px box); a ▶ at a hidden one brings it back (the maximized window steps back).
- `KioskStreamLayer` (a `ConsumerStatefulWidget` in the shell's stream slot) holds the windows' order, their corners and which one is maximized; `KioskStreamWindow` gains `onClose`, `reveal` (the latest ▶: the window expands and turns to it), `hidden`, `onMaximized`, `onFrame` and `onGesture`; its minimum width grows to 360 px for the extra button. A maximized window that collapses gives the area back. Switching between one window and several starts the windows afresh.
- **Overlapping windows on the web:** the engine keeps Flutter's canvases at `pointer-events: none` and only platform views answer, so a touch on a window's frame lying over another window's iframe would reach that iframe. Each window tells the layer what it covers (`onFrame`: its frame, the pill, the whole area, nothing while hidden — after each change, once no drag runs); a window whose frame lies under a later window's is `interactive: false`, and so is every player while any window is dragged or resized (`onGesture`).
- **Opened streams stay within the windows:** when the admin lowers „Oken najednou“ the oldest opened streams close; a stream whose slot is gone or lost its video takes no window and pushes no other out.
- **One video, several matches** (two matches streamed as one broadcast): its end closes every stream of it — a paging window would otherwise turn to the same, already ended player.
- **The Zápis button** sits in a strip of its own above the sheet: the sheet fills its box edge to edge, and a button over its top right corner hid the away team's totals.
- **The app's player stops its page when it goes:** webview_flutter_android leaves the native view to the garbage collector, so a closed window's stream would play on unseen and its channel call a closed window; `dispose` loads an empty page at the host origin and drops the channel.
- **A day's matches have the app's video control:** the dialog of a day's matches from the board's header showed the trophy at a match with a video; on the kiosk it now shows the app's own control (`MatchLeading`: the pulsing red camera while live, the play button otherwise) at a match with a YouTube video, and a tap closes the dialog and opens its stream window (`showDayMatchesDialog(onPlayVideo:)`, threaded like `onOpenMatch` through `KioskBoardView` and `BoardColumnHeader`). A video elsewhere than YouTube keeps the trophy; a tap on the row still opens the Zápis of a match with a score. The app's dialog (the browser) and the public overview (no links) are unchanged.
- **Every route above the kiosk switches the players off** (the user found the board's day dialog — its rows and „Zavřít“ — going to YouTube where it lay over a window): the shell counted the routes by hand (`KioskModals` around each `show…`) and the dialog of a day's matches was not counted. The shell now asks its own route (§5), so no dialog can be left out.

- **The live badge everywhere:** the drawer's match being played has the app's pulsing red camera (MatchLeading), not a ▶, like the day's list; a tap opens its stream. The admin's „Opakující se animace“ (`kiosk_animations`, 0065 — formerly „Dýchající tlačítko panelu“) now governs both the drawer button's breathing and the badge's pulse (`MatchLeading.pulse`, `showDayMatchesDialog(pulseLive:)`): off, the badge stays still — on a slow display every pulse repaints the screen. The ring sits in its own RepaintBoundary.

## Out of scope

Sound control from the kiosk, a stream for a match of another alley (league matches, 0055), persisting the window's place across restarts, any non-YouTube address, the public overview.
